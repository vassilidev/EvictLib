  • Favorites and albums are protectedItems you love or filed in an album stay out of every suggestion by default.

Get EvictLib.

Free and open source, not on the App Store yet. You install it once, from a computer.

Why a computer? The app comes as a file, EvictLib.ipa, and an iPhone can’t open it by itself. Sideloadly, a free app for Mac and Windows, signs it with your Apple ID and puts it on your iPhone through the cable.

You need an iPhone on iOS 17 or later, a Mac or Windows PC, a USB cable and your Apple ID.

  1. 1
    Download the appOne file, about 1.5 MB.EvictLib.ipa
  2. 2
    Install SideloadlyFree, from sideloadly.io. On Windows, also install iTunes from apple.com.
  3. 3
    Plug in your iPhoneUnlock it and tap Trust if it asks.
  4. 4
    Drop the file and click StartDrag EvictLib.ipa into Sideloadly, type your Apple ID email, click Start and sign in when asked.
  1. 1
    Clone and opengit clone https://github.com/vassilidev/EvictLib.git then open EvictLib.xcodeproj.
  2. 2
    Sign with your Apple IDTarget EvictLib › Signing & Capabilities: pick your team and set a unique bundle identifier.
  3. 3
    Run on your iPhoneSelect it as the destination and press ⌘R.

Then, on your iPhone

  1. 1
    Turn on Developer ModeSettings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode. The iPhone restarts; tap Turn On.
  2. 2
    Trust your Apple IDSettings › General › VPN & Device Management › your Apple ID › Trust.
  3. 3
    Open EvictLibAllow Full Access to Photos, then start with Largest Items.

Every 7 days: with a free Apple ID, Apple makes the app stop opening after a week. Plug in and click Start again in Sideloadly to renew it. Your photos are never affected.

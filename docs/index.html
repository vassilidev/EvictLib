EvictLib

Free up iCloud Photos space. No subscription, no quota.

Download for iPhone Source on GitHub

iOS 17 or later. Free and open source. Not on the App Store, see how to install.

Install

With a free Apple ID, Apple limits sideloaded apps to 7 days (re-sign to keep using it) and 3 apps at a time.

Without a Mac (Windows or Mac)

  1. Download EvictLib.ipa.
  2. Install Sideloadly (or AltStore).
  3. Plug in your iPhone, drop the IPA into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID, click Start.

With Xcode

  1. git clone https://github.com/vassilidev/EvictLib.git and open EvictLib.xcodeproj.
  2. Target EvictLib › Signing & Capabilities: pick your team, set a unique bundle identifier.
  3. Plug in your iPhone, select it, press ⌘R.

Then, on the iPhone

  1. Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode › On, then restart.
  2. Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › your Apple ID › Trust.
  3. Open EvictLib and allow Full Access to Photos.
